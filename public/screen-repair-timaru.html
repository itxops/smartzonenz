What a screen fault can look like

Visible cracks, black patches, coloured lines, flickering, dead touch areas or a display that stays blank can point to screen damage. A drop can also damage internal connections, so diagnosis helps identify the right repair.

What happens at the workshop

Bring the phone in for a free diagnosis. We inspect the reported issue, explain the available repair and quote, then proceed only after you approve. Ask us about the expected turnaround for your model.

Glass-only repair may suit some phones

When the display and touch layer remain in good condition, glass-only refurbishment may be an option for compatible models. It is not suitable for every phone; we check the panel before recommending it.

Before you visit

If the phone still works, back up important data and note any passcode or account requirements. Avoid using a phone with loose or sharp glass; cover it carefully for transport.
