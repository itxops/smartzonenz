Signs to get the battery checked

Shorter use between charges, unexpected shutdowns, inconsistent battery percentage or a phone that becomes unusually warm can have several causes. A diagnosis helps determine whether the battery, charger or another component is involved.

Free diagnosis and quote

Bring the phone in and describe what you have noticed. SmartZone checks the fault and explains the repair cost before any work begins. You can decide whether to go ahead.

Battery safety

If a phone is swelling, separating at the screen or becoming hot, stop charging it and keep it away from heat or pressure. Contact the workshop for advice on safe handling before bringing it in.

Keep your data backed up

A routine battery replacement is a hardware repair, but backing up important photos and files is sensible before any device service. Ask the technician if you have questions about your phone.
