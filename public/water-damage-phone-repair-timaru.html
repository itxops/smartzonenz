What to do first

Disconnect the charger and switch the phone off if you can do so safely. Remove the case and dry the outside with a soft cloth. Do not try to charge it or use heat to dry it.

Avoid rice and repeated testing

Rice does not remove liquid from inside a phone. Repeatedly powering on or charging a wet device can worsen electrical damage. Arrange an assessment as soon as practical.

What diagnosis can tell you

Liquid may reach the battery, display, charging path or logic board. Inspection helps identify affected areas and whether repair is practical; the outcome depends on the extent of the damage.

Protect important data

If the phone turns back on, back up important data when it is safe to do so. If it remains wet or behaves unusually, leave it powered off and seek repair advice.

Common questions

Water damage questions

Can a water-damaged phone be repaired?+

Sometimes. It depends on the liquid, how long the phone was exposed, whether it was powered or charged, and which components were affected. SmartZone can assess it and explain the options.

Should I put my wet phone in rice?+

No. Rice is not a reliable way to dry a phone internally. Turn the phone off, do not charge it, dry the exterior and arrange an assessment.
