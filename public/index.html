Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment before coming in?

No appointment is needed! You can walk in at 257 Stafford Street, Timaru anytime during our opening hours (Mon–Fri 9am–5:30pm, Sat 9:30am–4:30pm, Sun 10am–3pm).

How long does a phone screen or battery repair take?

Most standard screen and battery replacements are completed within 30 to 45 minutes right in our workshop. You can grab a coffee on Stafford Street and pick it up on your way back!

Can you replace just the outer glass instead of the full screen?

Yes! Where suitable, on compatible smartphones where the underlying OLED and touch are intact, glass-only refurbishment allows us to replace just the broken outer glass while preserving your original factory display. It saves money and reduces e-waste.

What if you can't fix my device?

No fix, no fee. If we can't resolve your hardware fault, you pay nothing on standard diagnosis.

Do you charge for diagnosis upfront?

No — diagnosis is 100% free ($0). We inspect your device, isolate the issue, and provide an upfront quote before any work begins. You decide whether to proceed.

Will I lose any photos, messages, or apps on my phone?

For standard hardware repairs (screens, batteries, charging ports, speakers, and camera lenses), your personal data is 100% untouched. We don't wipe your phone during hardware work.

Do you repair laptops, MacBooks, and gaming consoles?

Yes! We repair Apple MacBook Pro & Air, Dell, HP, Lenovo laptops, PlayStation 5, PS4, Xbox Series X, and Nintendo Switch for broken screens, batteries, HDMI ports, power circuits, and cooling fans.

What should I do if my phone was dropped in water?

Turn it off immediately and DO NOT plug it into a charger. Avoid placing it in rice (rice dust causes internal board corrosion). Bring it straight to 257 Stafford Street for ultrasonic cleansing and drying.
