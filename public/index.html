A Smarter Repair Option

Keep Your Original Display When The Device Allows It

Glass-only replacement isn't suitable for every phone or every type of damage. If the OLED/LCD, touch function or other components are damaged, a full screen replacement may be needed. We'll assess your device and explain the options before you decide.

GLASS

Glass-Only Screen Replacement

Where suitable, replace damaged outer glass while retaining the original display panel. Is the outer glass cracked but the display still working? On compatible devices, glass-only refurbishment may be an option instead of replacing the entire screen assembly.

  • Lower-cost alternative to a full display assembly We explain suitable repair options and transparent pricing upfront.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our repair process, turnaround times, and warranty.

How long does a phone screen or battery repair take?
Most standard screen and battery replacements are completed within 30 to 45 minutes right in our Timaru workshop. If you walk in, you can usually grab a coffee on Stafford Street and pick it up the same day!
Can you replace just the outer cracked glass instead of the entire screen?
Yes! Where suitable, on compatible smartphones and tablets where the underlying OLED/LCD and touch function remain undamaged, glass-only refurbishment allows us to replace just the broken outer glass while preserving your original factory display panel. It is a lower-cost, eco-friendly alternative to a full display assembly. Walk in for a $0 free diagnostic check to see if your screen qualifies.
Do I need to make an appointment before coming in?
No appointment is needed! You can walk in at 257 Stafford Street, Timaru anytime during our opening hours (Mon–Fri 9:00am–5:30pm, Sat 9:30am–4:30pm, Sun 10:00am–3:00pm). If you'd like to check parts availability for rare models first, call us on 03 927 2312.
How much will my repair cost?
Our initial diagnostic assessment is 100% free ($0). Once we examine your device and determine the fault, we provide a transparent, fixed price quote before any work starts. You decide whether to go ahead.
Will I lose any data, photos, or apps on my phone?
For standard hardware repairs (such as screens, batteries, charging ports, and camera replacements), your data is completely untouched. For major software recovery or severe motherboard faults, we always discuss backup options with you beforehand.
What should I do if my phone was dropped in water?
Turn it off immediately and DO NOT plug it into a charger. Avoid the rice trick, which causes internal corrosion. Bring it directly to 257 Stafford Street, Timaru. We open the device, perform an ultrasonic chemical wash to remove minerals, and dry all components.
Do you repair laptops and MacBooks as well?
Yes! We repair Apple MacBook Pro & Air laptops, Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, and Acer machines. We handle broken screens, battery swelling, charging socket repairs, SSD storage upgrades, and Windows / macOS reinstalls.
Do you repair gaming consoles like PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch?
Yes! We provide assessment and hardware repairs for supported gaming consoles. This includes replacing damaged HDMI ports (no signal on TV), fixing power supply faults and sudden game shutdowns, replacing faulty cooling fans and renewing thermal paste for overheating, and repairing Nintendo Switch USB-C ports, screens, and joy-cons. Bring your console into 257 Stafford Street, Timaru for a $0 free diagnosis!
