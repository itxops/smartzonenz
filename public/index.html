Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our repair turnaround times, pricing, and warranty.

How long does a phone screen or battery repair take?
Most standard screen and battery replacements are completed within 30 to 45 minutes right in our Timaru workshop. You can grab a coffee on Stafford Street and pick it up on your way back!
Can you replace just the outer cracked glass instead of the full screen?
Yes! Where suitable, on compatible smartphones where the underlying OLED/touch remains undamaged, glass-only refurbishment allows us to replace just the broken outer glass while preserving your original factory display panel. It is a lower-cost, eco-friendly option. Walk in for a $0 free diagnostic check.
Do I need an appointment before coming in?
No appointment is needed! You can walk in at 257 Stafford Street, Timaru anytime during our opening hours (Mon–Fri 9am–5:30pm, Sat 9:30am–4:30pm, Sun 10am–3pm).
How much will my repair cost?
Our initial diagnostic assessment is 100% free ($0). Once we test your device and isolate the fault, we quote a fixed, transparent price before any work begins. You decide whether to proceed.
Will I lose any photos, apps, or data on my phone?
For standard hardware repairs (screens, batteries, charging ports, speakers, and camera lenses), your personal data is 100% untouched. If complex software recovery is required, we discuss backup options with you beforehand.
What should I do if my phone was dropped in water?
Turn it off immediately and DO NOT plug it into a charger. Avoid placing it in rice (rice dust causes internal corrosion). Bring it to 257 Stafford Street right away for ultrasonic chemical wash and drying.
Do you repair laptops and MacBooks as well?
Yes! We repair Apple MacBook Pro & Air laptops, Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, and Acer machines. We replace broken screens, swollen batteries, charging sockets, SSD upgrades, and thermal paste servicing.
Do you repair gaming consoles like PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch?
Yes! We provide assessment and hardware repairs for PlayStation 5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch. This includes HDMI port replacements, power supply fixes, thermal cleaning for overheating consoles, and Switch joy-con / USB-C repairs.
